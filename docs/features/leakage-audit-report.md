## 1. Executive Summary

Temporal and target leakage is the most prevalent failure mode in meteorological machine learning. In weather forecasting, models that accidentally consume same-day ground observations or future error statistics exhibit artificially high validation scores that fail catastrophically in operational deployment.

Every feature implemented in `src/features` has been evaluated against three strict criteria:
1. **Temporal Availability at $T_0$:** Is this information genuinely accessible to an operational forecaster at 00:00 UTC on cycle date $T_0$?
2. **Target Isolation:** Is the verification label `obs_precip` completely separated from the feature predictor space $X$?
3. **Causal Error Lookback:** Does error memory restrict lookbacks to historical days strictly prior to $T_0$ ($\tau \ge 1\text{ day}$)?
