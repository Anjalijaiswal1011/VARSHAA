The National Meteorological Dilemma 02 / 07

Why Raw NWP Models Fail During Indian Extremes

Global models (GFS, NCUM, ECMWF) systematically under-predict flash floods and severe downpours.

🏔️ Complex Orography

25 km grid resolutions flatten the Western Ghats & Himalayan ridges, diluting forced orographic ascent and missing up to 160 mm of condensation.

☁️ Convective Smoothing

Sub-grid deep convective parameterization applies broad spatial smoothing, under-predicting the heavy tail (> 115.6 mm) by 35% to 55%.

⚠️ The Deterministic Trap

A single deterministic forecast provides zero uncertainty awareness to district magistrates, triggering false security before disasters.

Technical Innovation 03 / 07

RAAP-X: Physics-Guided Machine Learning Architecture

A three-stage post-processing pipeline maintaining atmospheric physics fidelity.

1. Synoptic Classifier
  • Dynamic 6-state synoptic classifier
  • Detects Active, Break, Depression & Western Disturbance regimes
  • Conditioned on 850 hPa vorticity & moisture convergence
2. Quantile Corrector
  • Gradient boosted multi-quantile regressors (P10, P50, P75, P90)
  • Rolling 1–14 day forecast-error memory
  • Sub-grid DEM slope & aspect vectors
3. Monotonicity Sorter
  • Mathematical enforcement of non-negativity (P ≥ 0)
  • Isotonic non-crossing sort (P10 ≤ P50 ≤ P75 ≤ P90)
  • Calibrated heavy (> 64.5 mm) & extreme (> 115.6 mm) probabilities
